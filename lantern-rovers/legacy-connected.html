GOOGLE PLAY ACCESS IN NEWER ANDROID BUILDS In builds carrying this access check, the app requests Google Play Age Signals API 0.0.4 solely for age-appropriate access and legal or regulatory compliance. Depending on Google Play's response, the app may temporarily receive an access status, lower and upper age-band bounds, an age-range source, parental significant-change approval status and date, and a Play install identifier. These raw responses stay in working memory only. We do not store or send their raw age bounds, source, approval date or install identifier to Firebase, advertising providers, analytics or our own server. We do not collect your date of birth, identity document, precise location or selfie for this check. Google Play handles its own account and verification processes under its own terms; an age range may be self-declared, parent-provided or verified by Google. Oreniq games does not independently verify your identity. Only a minimal access decision is kept on this device: permitted 13+ gameplay, 18+ connected-account eligibility, conservative offline-only play or a restriction, together with decision/check timestamps and policy/change revision markers. Allowed decisions are valid for at most 12 calendar months; clock rollback or inconsistent records can require another check. A known restriction is preserved until a valid compatible response resolves it. No raw age range, range source, parental approval date or Play install identifier is retained in this local decision. This is separate from the ordinary game save, Firebase account and optional activity outbox. At startup the app automatically checks when the saved decision is missing or unusable, or a previous permission has expired or is invalid for the current policy/change revision. A preserved restriction offers explicit retry. You can explicitly choose Check again to retry. Returning to the app enforces the existing decision; it does not automatically make a daily Store query. The age-access feature adds no automatic daily telemetry job. A successful NOT_SHARED response permits conservative ordinary offline play only when no known restriction takes precedence; it does not establish that you are an adult. A fresh error without a valid previous permission blocks play and offers retry. An unchanged, unexpired previous permission can permit ordinary offline play after an error, but cannot permit connected accounts, billing or other optional SDK flows on that error. Under-13, verification-required and unresolved parental change restrictions remain blocked. No advertising is enabled in this age-access QA flavor, and Store age information is not used to select, target or personalize ads. The newer Android build opens a connected tester account only after Google Play shares an age range establishing 18+ eligibility and you explicitly choose that feature. Email verification, your separate adult confirmation and the activity-sharing choice still apply. It does not automatically restore an account connection on first launch. The web hub's adult checkbox is self-attestation and does not independently verify age or replace the Android Store check. Declining optional activity sharing remains separate from age access. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove the local decision and restrictions along with local progress; a fresh installation must check again. It does not change your age or parental approval in Google Play, erase Google-held records, delete connected account data or guarantee unrestricted access. A policy notice cannot guarantee that another device, an earlier installed build, or an offline cached decision immediately learns a later Store change. Use Google Play and Check again to resolve an access problem; do not send identity documents or raw age responses to support. Earlier installed builds may require a successful online change to stop sharing; do not rely on the updated local withdrawal behavior until using an updated build. Removing a queue record does not securely erase independently retained exports or device backups. Messages you choose to send are separate from activity sharing and remain queued when sharing is withdrawn.
