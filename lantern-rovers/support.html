GOOGLE PLAY ACCESS IN NEWER ANDROID BUILDS The ordinary game remains intended for ages 13+. Only newer Android builds carrying the Store access check have this feature; a notice update does not change an earlier installed build. Check again retries an access check. A missing or unusable saved decision, or an expired or policy-invalid previous permission, is checked at startup, not by an automatic daily Store query. A preserved restriction offers explicit retry. An error with no valid previous permission offers retry; a valid unchanged previous permission can permit offline play only. NOT_SHARED can permit conservative ordinary offline play, but cannot establish 18+ account eligibility or remove a known restriction. Verification or parental-change restrictions must be resolved through Google Play; a retry cannot promise approval. Connected native accounts need a known 18+ Store range, your explicit choice, email verification and adult confirmation. The web hub's checkbox is separate self-attestation, not independent age verification. The device retains only a minimal access decision, timestamps and policy/change markers. Allowed decisions expire after at most 12 calendar months; restrictions remain until resolved. Raw Store age ranges, source, parental approval date and install identifier are not saved or sent to the hub, ads or analytics. Do not send date of birth, identity documents, selfies or raw age responses for support. Tell us your game version, the safe on-screen error and whether Google Play is installed and updated. Google Play handles its own verification. Clearing app data removes local progress and may remove local access decisions; it does not amend Store age/parental approval or delete connected data and is not an access workaround or routine recovery instruction. LOCAL SAVES Android Settings → Apps → Lantern Rovers → Storage → Clear data removes local progress. This is permanent unless you have a separate recoverable backup. Uninstalling also removes app-local data, but does not erase email, exports or device backups. Deleting a connected tester account does not delete the separate offline game save.
