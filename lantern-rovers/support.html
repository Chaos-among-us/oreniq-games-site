Lantern Rovers support and data requests Updated 8 October 2026 Oreniq games - Individual developer in Utah, United States Oreniqgames@gmail.com THIS GAMEPLAY TESTING VERSION Version 0.13.13 (code 51) needs no game account, connected feedback login or activity-sharing consent. A limited Google Play age and parental-approval check remains. Advertising and purchases are disabled. The game remains intended for ages 13 and older. BUG REPORTS If you are testing through Testers14, use its feedback channel. You can also email us. Describe what happened, what you expected and, if you choose, the version shown in Settings and your phone model. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending. The game does not automatically attach these details. Never send passwords, signing keys, payment details, identity documents or sensitive personal information. EXISTING TESTER ACCOUNTS AND DATA This update does not erase former tester accounts or server records. For access, correction, export, activity-only deletion or full account deletion, use the separate legacy hub: https://chaos-among-us.github.io/oreniq-games-site/lantern-rovers/testers/ Alternatively, email Oreniqgames@gmail.com with the subject 'Lantern Rovers data request', from the account email if possible. State whether you want activity records removed or the entire account deleted. Do not send identity documents. The legacy hub is not required to play this version; the separate Google Play access check may apply. Full account deletion covers tester messages, daily summaries, profile and Firebase sign-in account. If interrupted, the hub reports pending work and lets you resume; the disappearance of a profile alone does not prove completion. Turning sharing off does not delete earlier summaries. Earlier connected copies on other devices learn account changes only after successful sync and may have older withdrawal behavior. Existing retention commitments and provider backup limits remain described at: https://chaos-among-us.github.io/oreniq-games-site/lantern-rovers/legacy-connected.html GOOGLE PLAY ACCESS HELP If the game shows an access check, use Check again when available and follow Google Play's account verification or parental-approval steps. NOT_SHARED can permit ordinary gameplay without establishing adulthood, but does not override a known restriction. A fresh error with no valid previous permission blocks access; an unchanged valid prior permission may allow offline play after an error. Permitted local decisions expire after at most 12 calendar months. There is no automatic daily Store query or attendance upload. A retry cannot promise approval. For help, describe the safe on-screen error and game version if you choose. Do not send birth dates, identity documents, selfies or raw age responses. Clearing storage can erase progress and local decisions, but does not change Google Play's age or parental information. LOCAL SAVES Keep the app installed and update through Google Play to preserve ordinary progress. Do not clear storage as a routine fix. Android Settings > Apps > Lantern Rovers > Storage > Clear data can permanently remove progress and other private local files. Uninstalling removes app-local data but does not erase cloud accounts, emails, independent exports or device backups. Account deletion does not delete the separate offline save. TESTING REPORTS Testers14 advertises daily activity reporting and feedback. Those reports come from its service; this version contains no provider attendance tracker. A missing or present dashboard entry does not independently prove how long someone played or their Google Play eligibility. Report honest failures and confusing gameplay as well as successes.
