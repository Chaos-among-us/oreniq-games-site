Lantern Rovers privacy policy Updated 22 September 2026 Publisher and privacy contact: Oreniq games, Oreniqgames@gmail.com. Oreniq games is the publishing name of an individual developer based in Utah, United States. THIS VERSION This policy describes the ordinary offline game and a separate, connected tester program. The ordinary game remains intended for players ages 13 and older. Connected accounts are limited to invited adults aged 18 or older. The connected tester service and any sample ads or billing checks are restricted to a separate tester build. PROGRESS ON YOUR DEVICE The ordinary game stores progress, equipment, inventory, preferences, tutorial completion, rewards and any expedition in progress in private app storage. There is no cloud save for ordinary play and the game does not upload ordinary-player attendance. Device backup or transfer features may retain their own copies under your device settings. The tester account and its outbox are separate from the offline save. OPTIONAL EMAIL FEEDBACK If you open an email feedback draft, it contains your written comments, game version, device model and operating-system description. You can edit or delete these details before sending. Your chosen email app handles sending. If you send the email, Oreniq games receives your sender address and the content you include and uses them to answer support questions, investigate bugs and improve the game. Do not include passwords, payment information or sensitive personal details. Email providers also process messages under their own policies. SHARING Optional progress sharing prepares text with your wins and unlocked level. You choose the receiving app and whether to send. The game does not read your contacts or send invitations automatically. The receiving service handles shared information under its own policy. CONNECTED TESTER ACCOUNTS A separate tester build and web hub offer account enrollment for adults aged 18 or older. We ask you to enter an email address, password and display alias, verify your email, confirm you are an adult, and make a separate choice about optional daily activity summaries. We do not ask for your birth date or identity document; adult status is self-confirmed, not independently verified. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in, password reset and email verification. It assigns an account identifier and processes your email address and password. We receive the verified email address, Firebase account identifier and verification status, but not your password. Firebase may process IP address and user-agent information for account security and abuse prevention. Our Firestore profile stores your email, alias, adult confirmation, activity-sharing choice, privacy notice version, creation time, pending or approved status, and a separate access-provisioned status. These access fields do not prove Google Play opt-in, installation or eligibility. PRIVATE TESTER FEEDBACK After approval, you can send written feedback through the tester build or hub and read personal replies from Oreniq games. The private conversation stores message text, conversation topic and identifier, sender role, message identifier, server receipt time and game build/version. Only you and the authorized test owner can read the conversation. Messages are not public or testimonials. Attachments are not accepted. Please do not include passwords, payment details, precise location, health information or other sensitive personal information. OPTIONAL DAILY ACTIVITY You may decline or later withdraw permission to share activity and still use the tester account and game. When enabled, the tester build records only foreground expedition time while the app is focused and unpaused and there has been recent meaningful input. It groups reports by UTC date and sends aggregate active seconds, kills, gathering nodes, lantern/wave progress, completed runs and game version. The test's qualifying-day measure is at least 120 active expedition seconds and at least one kill, node or lantern event. A login or daily gift does not qualify. These summaries are generated on your device and are not independently verified. Device clock changes, offline play, interruptions, storage limits and sync failures can make a report incomplete or place it on an uncertain date. A missing report does not prove that you did not play. Activity is a diagnostic; it does not prove attention, enjoyment, motivation, continued Google Play opt-in or Play eligibility. We do not rank testers or require daily play, positive feedback or favorable reviews. With permission enabled, reports are queued on the device and sent over an encrypted connection when available. The app keeps up to 50 unsent messages and 32 recent daily summaries; older activity may be dropped and flagged as missing. If sharing is turned off, new collection and uploads stop. Previously uploaded summaries remain until account deletion; unsent summaries are not uploaded after withdrawal. To keep you signed in, the Android tester build stores an encrypted Firebase refresh credential in private app storage protected by Android Keystore and excluded from Android backup. It does not save your password. Signing out removes the saved credential; the server still verifies account access. Credentials are never included in the feedback outbox. OWNER'S TEST EXCHANGE CHECKLIST The owner can keep a private reciprocal-testing checklist in the hub: app names, developer aliases, public app/group/contact links, testing instructions, dates, manual play and feedback checkmarks, and brief notes. It may link to an existing tester account identifier to display that tester's optional Lantern Rovers activity reports. These records are stored in Firebase and accessible only to the authorized owner. This does not monitor other apps or collect their usage, and a missing Lantern report is not proof of no play. Notes should not contain passwords or sensitive personal details. The owner deletes exchange records no later than 90 days after that exchange ends, or earlier on request to Oreniqgames@gmail.com; this is owner-managed cleanup, not an automatic timer. Account deletion also requires the owner to remove any related exchange notes or links. HOW WE USE INFORMATION Oreniq games uses tester information to review applications, manage test access, provide support, answer feedback, investigate problems and improve the game. Optional activity summaries help us understand testing progress and reliability. We do not sell tester information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not use Google Analytics for this program. Access to private tester records is limited to the authorized test owner. ADVERTISING The separate tester build may offer an optional Google rewarded test ad. It uses Google's sample rewarded-ad unit and Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent services; it is not a production placement and is not intended to generate ad revenue. You can continue playing without an ad, and normal rewards are not withheld for declining. When you choose the ad flow, Google may process device or advertising identifiers, IP and network information, privacy choices, and ad request or interaction information to provide the SDK, consent form and test ad. Processing is subject to Google's privacy information and can vary with Android, SDK configuration and your choices. This tester configuration is not approval for production advertising. TEST BILLING QA The separate tester build may expose Google Play billing checks for the one-time Nightfall and Dawn appearance products. These non-consumable appearance products are for restricted billing QA with designated Google Play license testers and Google's test payment methods. Testers do not need to spend money. Do not start a purchase unless the owner has confirmed license-tester access and the Google Play checkout identifies a test purchase; stop if a real charge may occur. Google Play processes checkout and payment details. The game does not receive card or bank details. It stores product identifiers and signed purchase/ownership records locally to restore the test appearance. No live price, public offer or commercial-sale policy is established by this QA. AGE GROUPS The ordinary game is intended for players ages 13 and older. The connected tester account, inbox and activity service are limited to adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly enroll people under 18 in the connected program or ask them to provide account information. The adult confirmation is not an age-verification process. Parents or guardians may contact Oreniq games if a minor's information was submitted so we can review and delete it. RETENTION, DELETION AND SECURITY Tester accounts, Firestore profiles, messages and activity summaries are deleted as part of owner-managed cleanup no later than 90 days after the test ends, or earlier when an account holder requests deletion through the hub. A deletion request stops new account activity and begins removal of messages, summaries, profile and Firebase Authentication account. If deletion is interrupted, the interface reports that it is pending and allows the account holder to resume; do not assume uninstalling the game or clearing local storage removes cloud records. A minimal random Firebase UID and server deletion time may remain in a deleted-account marker for up to 90 days after Auth deletion to block stale sessions from recreating the profile and to finish cleanup. This marker contains no email, alias, messages or activity. Google may retain service-level backups and security logs under its own schedules. Firebase states that authentication IP logs are retained for a few weeks and other authentication information is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is requested. These provider copies are not controlled by an app-level timer. Local progress and device backups are separate from tester account deletion. We limit access to tester records and no storage or transmission method is guaranteed completely secure. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND WEBSITE The connected service uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, provided by Google. The default Firestore database is in the United States multi-region nam5; Firebase Authentication processes data in the United States. Google may process data elsewhere when a service does not offer a location choice. Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy . Firestore location details: https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/locations . Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . The Oreniq games hub is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses and request information for security and service operation. The hub does not add Google Analytics or separate web analytics. GitHub's privacy statement is at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . YOUR CHOICES AND CHANGES Feedback and sharing are optional. You can play the ordinary game without a tester account. Connected testers can turn activity sharing off, download their records, or request account deletion through the hub or by contacting Oreniqgames@gmail.com. You may have additional privacy rights where you live. Material changes to collection will be explained before new collection begins. The dated policy is available from Settings → Privacy, terms & credits. OPTIONAL TESTER REFERRALS Approved testers can share a personal invitation link containing a pseudonymous code derived from their tester account identifier. It contains no email address or password. Applicants may keep or clear the optional invitation code before submitting their application. We store the code with the application, whether it has been credited, and the inviter's earned badge count (0 to 3). The owner uses these records to verify genuine new participants, prevent duplicate rewards and award cosmetic tester badges. The inviter sees their own badge count, not the recruit's email, identity, feedback or activity. The owner can confirm an expedition through a private conversation, so activity sharing is never required. These records follow the same account deletion and 90-days-after-test retention policy. Codes in links may be visible to the people or services you choose to share them with. They grant no account or Google Play access.
