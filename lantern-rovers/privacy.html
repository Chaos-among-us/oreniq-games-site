Lantern Rovers privacy policy Updated 21 September 2026 Publisher and privacy contact: Oreniq games, Oreniqgames@gmail.com. Oreniq games is the publishing name of an individual developer based in Utah, United States. THIS VERSION This policy describes the ordinary offline development/playtest version. It does not describe a future online tester service or a live advertising release. Separate engineering builds marked sample-ads connect to Google's services and are not the ordinary player build. PROGRESS ON YOUR DEVICE The game stores progress, equipment, inventory, preferences, tutorial completion, rewards and any expedition in progress in its private app storage. A backup copy helps recover interrupted saves. There is no game account or game-operated cloud-save service. The game does not upload an attendance history. Device/operating-system backup and transfer features may retain their own copies under your device settings. OPTIONAL EMAIL FEEDBACK If you open an email feedback draft, it contains your written comments, game version, device model and operating-system description. You can edit or delete these details before sending. Your chosen email app handles sending. If you send the email, Oreniq games receives your sender address and the content you include and uses them to answer support questions, investigate bugs and improve the game. Do not include passwords, payment information or sensitive personal details. Email providers also process messages under their own policies. SHARING Optional progress sharing prepares text with your wins and unlocked level. You choose the receiving app and whether to send. The game does not read your contacts or send invitations automatically. The receiving service handles shared information under its own policy. ADVERTISING AND PURCHASES This ordinary version does not request or display advertisements and has no purchases. Google advertising software is included but its ad initialization and request path are disabled. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, cloud diagnostics and purchasing services are disabled in the game configuration. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access. We will update disclosures and any required consent choices before enabling advertising or online services. TESTER INBOX PREVIEW Only a separately provisioned development installation exposes the local tester conversation. It stores a private tester alias, written messages, message identifiers and times, game version and imported replies in app storage. It does not send messages online or automatically record daily play. A developer may manually transfer the conversation for a test with the participant's knowledge. A future connected service will need separate enrollment and disclosures before collection begins. Ordinary installations have no provisioned tester identity. AGE GROUPS AND PARENTS Lantern Rovers is designed and marketed for teens and adults ages 13 and older. This is our intended audience, not an assigned content rating. We do not invite children under 13 into online testing or knowingly collect their personal information for advertising. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete it. Offline gameplay does not require a name, birthday, email address or account. Children should ask a parent or guardian to handle support and external sharing. Parents can contact us about information a child has sent. No child's information is used by us for targeted advertising in this version. Audience-specific advertising and online feedback are not enabled by this policy. RETENTION, DELETION AND SECURITY Local progress stays until the app's storage is cleared or the app is removed; device backups and separately exported files may remain. Clearing storage removes progress and cannot be undone by us. To request access, correction or deletion of support messages or manually provided tester records, email Oreniqgames@gmail.com from the address you used, or provide the tester alias if applicable. Do not send identity documents. We retain support information only while needed to handle the request, resolve associated issues, or meet applicable legal obligations; we review stored records and delete information no longer needed. Some residual provider backups may expire on that provider's schedule. We restrict access to support records and use device and service access controls. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed completely secure. WEBSITE HOSTING The Oreniq games website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. These static Lantern Rovers pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms or cookies. GitHub describes its own processing at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Reading the bundled pages inside the game does not open the website. YOUR CHOICES AND CHANGES Feedback and sharing are optional. You can play without them. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, including a right to complain to the relevant privacy regulator. Contact the address above to exercise a right or ask a question. Material changes to collection will be explained before the new feature is enabled. The dated policy is available from Settings → Privacy, terms & credits.
