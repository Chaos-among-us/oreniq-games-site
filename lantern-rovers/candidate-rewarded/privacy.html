GOOGLE PLAY AGE AND PARENTAL ACCESS On supported Android builds the game requests Google Play Age Signals API 0.0.4 for age-appropriate access and legal or regulatory compliance. Depending on the response, it may temporarily receive access status, lower and upper age-band bounds, age-range source, significant-change parental approval status and date, and a Play install identifier. These raw fields stay in working memory. We do not save them or send them to Firebase, our own server, advertising, analytics or Testers14. We do not ask for date of birth, identity documents, precise location or selfies. Google handles its account and verification processes under its own terms. An age range may be self-declared, parent-provided or verified by Google; we do not independently verify identity. A minimal access decision, decision/check timestamps and policy/change revision markers are kept in private storage separately from the game save. A permitted decision lasts at most 12 calendar months; clock rollback or inconsistent records may require a new check. A known restriction remains until a valid compatible response resolves it. Raw age bounds, source, parental approval date and Play install identifier are not stored in that decision. The game checks at startup when a saved decision is missing or unusable, or permission is expired or invalid for the current policy/change revision. A preserved restriction offers an explicit retry, and you may choose Check again. Returning to the app enforces the existing decision; this adds no automatic daily Store query or attendance report. A successful NOT_SHARED response may allow conservative ordinary gameplay when no known restriction takes precedence. A fresh error without valid prior permission blocks access and offers retry. An unchanged, unexpired prior permission may allow offline gameplay after an error. Under-13, verification-required and unresolved parental-change restrictions remain blocked until resolved through the appropriate Google Play process. Optional ads do not override those restrictions. This release has no game tester accounts and no account enrollment age requirement. Store access data is not used to personalize advertising or create a marketing profile. Clearing storage or uninstalling may remove the local decision and game progress; it does not change age or parental approval in Google Play or guarantee unrestricted access. A fresh installation checks again. An older installed copy or offline cached decision cannot immediately learn every later Store change. Do not send raw age responses or identity documents to support. OPTIONAL REWARDED ADS An eligible expedition result may offer an extra bundle of Coins and materials. The prompt shows that bundle before you choose Watch ad. Ordinary rewards are kept if you decline, an ad is unavailable or it fails. There are no automatic banner or interstitial advertisements. No purchase, favorable review, referral or marketing subscription is needed for the reward. An internet connection is needed for an ad. AD ELIGIBILITY AND CHOICES Optional ads are available only from age 15, subject to Google Play restrictions and privacy choices. A neutral local choice offers Under 15, 15-17, 18 or older, and Prefer not to say. Under 15, no choice or declining means the ad and consent services are not contacted. The same extra-reward calculation and limits apply to every eligible player. We do not ask for a birth date or identity documents, and this local answer is not independently verified identity. Google AdMob supplies optional ads. We use the same teen-protective, G-rated, nonpersonalized request treatment for all ad-eligible players, including adults. Ads are not selected using a behavioral marketing profile supplied by the game. Before contacting ad services, we explain the Google data use and offer Agree and continue or Not now. Regional provider privacy choices follow where applicable. You may decline and keep ordinary expedition rewards. Turning ads off or withdrawing the local choice is available in Settings. Ad privacy choices provides the applicable provider form when available and eligible. Ads stay unavailable if current-session consent checks fail or applicable device-storage permission is missing. A provider permission flag alone is not a replacement for the game's age and privacy safeguards. The game keeps a small ad-preference record in private device storage, separately from ordinary progress: the selected age band or unknown/declined choice, a device-clock timestamp for reviewing that choice, whether ad services were previously requested, and a single derived flag recording a known Store restriction on ads. It keeps no birth date, raw Store age ranges or status, account details or Play install identifier in that record. The preference remains until changed or app storage is removed. You can turn ads off immediately; otherwise the age preference can be reviewed after 24 hours. The prior-use flag helps keep withdrawal controls reachable; the restriction flag prevents a later ambiguous response from overriding a known restriction. Neither is an attendance record. The explicit ad-data acknowledgment is held only in memory for the current session and is requested again after a non-ad background transition or a privacy review. The derived current-session Store ad-eligibility result is also memory-only; the separate restrictive flag described above may persist. Ads require a fresh Store response in the current session or after resuming. A valid NOT_SHARED response can use an eligible local choice only when no prior restrictive Store result takes precedence. Transport errors or cached ordinary-gameplay permission alone never allow ads. Raw Store fields and identifiers are not saved or forwarded to advertising. The game does not send the chosen age band, birth date, player save, support email or legacy tester identity to the ad service. A common protective ad-treatment flag does not identify the player or certify their age. When ad/consent services are contacted, Google and its advertising partners may process the IP address (including approximate location), ad/app interactions, performance and diagnostic data, and device or app identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Removing or limiting advertising identifiers does not make an ad data-free. Our release configuration does not request the Android advertising-ID permission; other provider identifiers and network information may still be processed. No precise-location permission is requested for ads. Provider privacy choices and applicable restrictions affect which data is used. Google describes its practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy . Provider processing, retention and requests are also governed by those notices; deleting this game cannot delete every record held by a provider. We do not operate a separate gameplay analytics or ad-profile server. Google acts under its own applicable privacy and advertising terms; its processing is not all performed solely on our instructions. REWARDED-AD RECORDS ON YOUR DEVICE The game stores a pending reward offer, local completion/recovery records and claim-limit timing in private app storage, separately from and alongside ordinary progress. Clearing storage or uninstalling may remove the reward record and progress. A reward has no cash value or cash-out right.
